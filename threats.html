Emerging AI Threats

Traditional security awareness is no longer enough. We analyze the three main AI-driven vectors that are bypassing modern firewalls and targeting organizations like ARUP.

DEEPFAKE ATTACKS

What Is It?

Hyper-realistic video and audio forgeries created by AI to impersonate executives or public figures. These fake recordings can appear highly believable in business and media settings.

How Attackers Use It?

  • Video Calls: Faking a CFO on a live Zoom call to authorize transfers.
  • Disinformation: Spreading fake news to damage reputation.

REAL IMPACT

In 2024, an engineering firm lost

£20 MILLION

after employees were tricked by a deepfake video conference.

AI PHISHING

What Is It?

Scam emails generated by Large Language Models (LLMs) that are grammatically perfect and highly personalized. These messages can sound professional, making them harder to recognise.

How Attackers Use It?

  • Spoofing Style: AI mimics your boss's exact writing tone.
  • Bypassing Filters: No spelling mistakes means it lands in your inbox.

REAL IMPACT

AI phishing has a

98% BYPASS RATE

against traditional spam filters, putting every employee at risk.

VOICE CLONING

What Is It?

Synthetic audio created from just a few seconds of sample audio, used to impersonate targets over the phone. This makes voice-based scams more convincing and harder to detect quickly.

How Attackers Use It?

  • Vishing: Faking a family member in distress to demand money.
  • MFA Bypass: Fooling bank voice authentication systems.

REAL IMPACT

A grandmother was tricked into sending cash after receiving a distress call from an AI clone of her grandson's voice.

EXAMPLE: AI PHISHING EMAIL SCENARIO

From: Michael Ross (CEO) m.ross@cybersentinel-corp.com

To: Finance Team

Subject: URGENT: Wire Transfer for Project X - Immediate Action Required

Hi Team,

I need you to process the attached invoice immediately. We are at risk of losing the contract if this isn’t settled by EOD today. The vendor has updated their banking details, so please use the account information listed in the attached PDF.

This takes priority over everything else. Let me know once it's done.

Thanks,

Michael

WARNING SIGNS & ANALYSIS

  • Unusual Urgency: Phrases like “Immediate Action Required” and “risk of losing contract” are pressure tactics designed to stop you from thinking carefully.
  • New Banking Details: A request to change payment details is a major sign of CEO fraud. This should always be checked by a phone call.
  • AI Tone: The grammar is perfect and professional. Traditional spam filters often look for mistakes, so this type of email can bypass them.

Key Warning Signs of AI-Driven Threats

The points below summarise common signs that may help users recognise different types of AI-based cyber threats.

  • Deepfake Attacks
    • Unnatural facial expressions
    • Voice and lip movement do not fully match
    • Video or audio quality seems suspicious
  • AI Phishing
    • Urgent language that pressures the user
    • Fake links or suspicious email addresses
    • Requests for passwords or personal details
  • Voice Cloning
    • Unexpected calls asking for money
    • A familiar voice sounding slightly unusual
    • Pressure to act quickly without checking
