DEFENCE STRATEGIES YOU CAN USE

Defending Against Deepfakes

Video calls are no longer proof of identity. If you are asked to transfer money or share sensitive info on a video call, follow these steps:

✔ Establish a “Safe Word” or Question

Agree on a secret word or question (e.g., “What was the name of your first pet?”) with your family or finance team. Ask this if the request feels sudden.

✔ Look for Visual Glitches

AI video can look real, but often has small errors in blinking, lip movement, or skin texture around the mouth and eyes.

✔ Verify via a second channel

If a “CEO” calls you on Zoom/Teams, pause the video and call them on their known mobile number to confirm.

WHY THIS MATTERS?

Deepfake technology can appear realistic, so checking identity in another way helps prevent fraud.

Spotting AI Phishing

AI writes perfect emails without spelling mistakes. Do not rely on grammar to spot scams. Focus on the context and the links.

✔ Check the Actual Email Address

Ignore the “Display Name” (e.g., “Michael Ross”). Click or hover on the sender’s name to see the real address (e.g., m.ross@fake-company.com).

✔ Hover Before Clicking

Hover your mouse over any button or link without clicking. A small box will appear at the bottom left showing the real website URL.

✔ Distrust "Urgency"

Phrases like “Immediate Action Required” or “Within the hour” are psychological pressure tactics. Real business is rarely that sudden.

WHY THIS MATTERS?

AI can copy your boss's writing style perfectly. Even if the tone sounds right, if the request is unusual or urgent, you must verify the source.

Stopping Voice Scams

Voice cloning only needs 3 seconds of audio. Never trust a caller ID, as it can be easily faked (“Spoofed”).

✔ Hang Up and Call Back

If someone calls claiming to be from your bank or a family member in trouble, hang up. Call them back on a number you know is real.

✔ Ask a "Memory Question."

Ask the caller something only the real person would know (e.g., “Where did we go for dinner last year?”). AI cannot easily access personal shared memories.

✔ Never Share Personal Info Via Phone

Legitimate organizations will never ask for passwords, PINs, or full credit card numbers over an incoming phone call.

WHY THIS MATTERS?

Voice biometrics used by banks are being tricked. Taking control of the conversation and verifying facts yourself is the safest way to stop voice fraud.

Why These Strategies Matter?

Tools like our simulator help users practise, but in the real world, the strongest defence is awareness, critical thinking, and responsible online behaviour. By following these specific steps for Video, Email, and Voice, users can identify suspicious warning signs, reduce the risk of manipulation, and make safer decisions before responding to unexpected requests. These strategies matter because AI-powered cyber threats can appear highly realistic and professional, so verifying information carefully and using trusted communication methods can help protect both personal and organisational security.
