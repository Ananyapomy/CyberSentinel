AI Powered Cyber Threats

Artificial Intelligence has created powerful tools for innovation, but it has also introduced new cyber threats that are harder to detect

Threat

Deepfake Attacks

AI-generated video and audio can impersonate executives and manipulate employees during meetings and financial requests

Threat

Phishing

AI can generate convincing phishing emails and fake messages that trick users into clicking links or sharing sensitive data

Threat

Voice Cloning

Attackers can replicate someone's voice using AI and perform fraudulent phone calls to request sensitive information
